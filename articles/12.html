Huawei’s Tri-Fold Mate XT: A Pricey Foldable That Attracts Over 5 Million Pre-Orders

Huawei has once again pushed the boundaries of smartphone innovation with the launch of its first triple-folding phone, the Mate XT. This new device comes with a premium price tag, starting at S$3,660 (19,999 yuan) for the 256GB model, making it S$1,000 more expensive than the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 6.

A Premium Device with Impressive Features

For those who need more storage, the Mate XT is also available in a 512GB version priced at 21,999 yuan (around S$4,025) and a top-tier 1TB version for 23,999 yuan (about S$4,390). Regardless of storage, all models boast 16GB of RAM and come in two stunning colors: black and red.

The Mate XT’s standout feature is its unique dual-hinge design that allows both inward and outward folding, providing exceptional flexibility. The device can transform into different modes: a full 10.2-inch tablet when fully unfolded, a compact 7.9-inch screen when partially folded, and a more traditional 6.4-inch smartphone when completely collapsed.

Powerful Specs Packed Inside

Under the hood, the Mate XT doesn’t compromise. The device sports a vibrant OLED touchscreen with a 240Hz touch sampling rate, ensuring smooth performance. It’s backed by a large 5,600mAh battery that supports both 66W wired fast charging and 50W wireless charging, keeping the power-hungry screen going all day long.

Camera enthusiasts won’t be disappointed either, as the phone is equipped with a 50MP ultra-optical variable camera, a 12MP ultra-wide camera, and a 12MP periscope telephoto lens on the back. For selfies, an 8MP front camera does the job.

Over 5 Million Pre-Orders Despite the Price

Despite its eye-watering price and the fact that it’s only available in China for now, the Mate XT has garnered massive interest. According to reports, over 5 million people have already placed pre-orders for the device through Chinese retailer Vmall.

It’s clear that Huawei’s foldable phone is creating a buzz, appealing to consumers who are eager to experience the future of mobile technology.
