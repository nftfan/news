10 Reasons to Wait for Next Year's iPhone 17

Apple's iPhone development roadmap runs several years into the future, and the company is continually working with suppliers on successive iPhone models simultaneously. This allows for feature leaks long before official announcements. The iPhone 17 series, set to release in 2025, is no exception.

Here are 10 rumored features expected to arrive with the iPhone 17 series:

1. Under-Display Face ID

The iPhone 17 Pro is rumored to be the first iPhone to feature under-display Face ID technology, leaving only a small circular cutout for the front-facing camera. A fully all-screen design is expected by 2027.

2. New Display Sizes

The iPhone 17 and iPhone 17 Plus will introduce new display dimensions. Apple's standard models could now offer a 6.27-inch screen, with a potential change in display size for the iPhone 17 Plus.

3. 120Hz ProMotion with Always-on Display

ProMotion is rumored to come to the standard iPhone models, offering a 120Hz refresh rate and always-on display functionality, enabling the Lock Screen to show the clock, notifications, and widgets when the device is locked.

4. Apple-Designed Wi-Fi 7 Chip

The iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max could feature Apple’s own Wi-Fi 7 chip, allowing faster speeds, lower latency, and reducing reliance on external suppliers like Broadcom.

5. 48MP Telephoto Lens

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is expected to have an upgraded 48MP telephoto lens, optimized for use with Apple’s Vision Pro headset, making it the first iPhone with all 48MP rear cameras.

6. 24MP Selfie Camera

All iPhone 17 models will feature a 24MP front-facing camera, improving image quality and detail for selfies, with an upgraded six-element lens.

7. Scratch Resistant Anti-Reflective Display

iPhone 17 models will likely feature a more scratch-resistant anti-reflective display, using a super-hard anti-reflective layer to improve durability.

8. More Memory

The iPhone 17 Pro models may come with 12GB of RAM, offering improved multitasking and memory capacity, compared to the current 6GB or 8GB in the iPhone 15 series.

9. Smaller Dynamic Island

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is expected to feature a smaller Dynamic Island due to a narrower Face ID "metalens" system.

10. iPhone 17 "Slim"

A new "iPhone 17 Slim" model could offer a thinner design with a single rear camera and aluminum chassis. This device might replace the iPhone 17 Plus.
