AirPods 4 Introduce Hidden Capacitive Button for Pairing

NFTFANS TOKEN | PUBLISHED ON 12 September 2024 | Author: NFTFANS

Apple's fourth-generation AirPods have taken a notable step forward in design by removing the traditional Setup Button found on previous models. Instead of the physical button, the new AirPods 4 feature a discreet capacitive button located on the front of the Charging Case.

This hidden button simplifies the pairing and resetting processes. To enter pairing mode, users need to open the Charging Case and perform a double tap on the front where the hidden control is located. For factory resets, a triple tap or a long press and hold gesture activates the reset function.

The status light on the AirPods 4 Charging Case is now more subtle. There is no visible LED dot when the case is not in use or closed. The light only becomes visible through the white plastic when the case is opened or placed on a charger.

The AirPods 4 come in two variants: one with Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) and one without. Starting at $129, they are now available for pre-order and are scheduled to launch on Friday, September 20.

For those interested, the new AirPods 4 offer a modernized experience with enhanced functionality and a sleek design, making them a notable upgrade in Apple's wireless earbuds lineup.
